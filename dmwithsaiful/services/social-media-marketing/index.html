Social Media and Channels · Service 04 of 11

Social Media Marketing Strategy for Your Brand

Social media marketing decides who you speak to, what you stand for and how your message travels across channels. We build the strategy and campaign plan; day-to-day publishing is a separate service.

Enquire about Social Media Marketing →WhatsApp

Who it is for

Brands launching a campaign, entering new channels or unsure how to position themselves.

Problems it addresses

  • No defined audience or message
  • Posting everywhere without a channel plan
  • Campaigns that start without measurable goals

Scope

What you get

  1. Audience and competitor review
  2. Positioning and message framework
  3. Channel selection and campaign strategy
  4. Distribution plan across organic and paid channels
  5. Goal and measurement plan

Workflow

How it works with you

You stay involved at each stage and approve the work before it goes live.

  1. Understand

    We learn about your business, audience and goals first.

  2. Plan

    We agree priorities, scope and a calendar with you.

  3. Create & Launch

    We produce the agreed work and publish it with your approval.

  4. Review & Improve

    We report what happened and use it to improve the next cycle.

What we need from you

  • Business goals and offers
  • Existing social accounts and any performance data
  • Brand guidelines, if you have them

Reporting and review

You receive a written strategy, then reviews at agreed points against the goals set in the plan.
