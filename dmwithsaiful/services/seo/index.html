SEO Services to Help Customers Find Your Website

Search engine optimisation improves how clearly your website answers the questions people type into search. We start with an audit, map your pages to real search needs and make practical, prioritised changes.

Enquire about SEO →WhatsApp

Who it is for

Businesses with a website or online shop that want steadier visibility in search without paying for every visit.

Problems it addresses

  • Pages that do not match what customers search for
  • Titles, headings and descriptions that are missing or duplicated
  • Technical issues that stop pages being found or loading quickly

Scope

What you get

  1. Website SEO audit with prioritised findings
  2. Keyword mapping: one main topic per page
  3. On-page improvements to titles, headings, descriptions and internal links
  4. Technical recommendations for your developer or site owner
  5. Content planning for pages and articles that fill search gaps

Workflow

How it works with you

You stay involved at each stage and approve the work before it goes live.

  1. Understand

    We learn about your business, audience and goals first.

  2. Plan

    We agree priorities, scope and a calendar with you.

  3. Create & Launch

    We produce the agreed work and publish it with your approval.

  4. Review & Improve

    We report what happened and use it to improve the next cycle.

What we need from you

  • Website access or a developer contact
  • Search Console access, or help setting it up
  • Details of your products, services and customers

Reporting and review

A monthly report explains what was changed, what has moved in Search Console data and the next priorities. Rankings can rise or fall for reasons outside our control, so we report progress rather than promise positions.

How long does SEO take?

Changes are often picked up over weeks or months, depending on your site and competition. We do not promise timings or rankings.

Do you write the new content?

Content planning is included. Full writing can be added through Copywriting.

Is this the same as Google Ads?

No. SEO improves organic visibility, while Google Ads is paid placement. They can support each other.
