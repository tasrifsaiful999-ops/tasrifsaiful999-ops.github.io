Meta Ads Management for Facebook and Instagram

Meta ads let you pay to reach defined audiences on Facebook and Instagram. We plan the goal and audience, set up campaigns in Meta Ads Manager, test creative and report on what the spend achieved.

Enquire about Meta Ads →WhatsApp

Who it is for

Businesses that sell or take enquiries online and want measured paid social campaigns.

Problems it addresses

  • Boosting posts without a clear objective
  • Audiences that are too broad or too narrow
  • Creative that is never tested or refreshed

Scope

What you get

  1. Goal setting and audience research
  2. Campaign structure and setup in Meta Ads Manager
  3. Creative briefs and coordination with your assets
  4. Testing of audiences, creatives and copy
  5. Optimisation and regular reporting

Workflow

How it works with you

You stay involved at each stage and approve the work before it goes live.

  1. Understand

    We learn about your business, audience and goals first.

  2. Plan

    We agree priorities, scope and a calendar with you.

  3. Create & Launch

    We produce the agreed work and publish it with your approval.

  4. Review & Improve

    We report what happened and use it to improve the next cycle.

What we need from you

  • Access to your ad account and Facebook Page
  • Offer, product and brand details
  • A budget you decide
  • Creative files, or a plan to source them

Reporting and review

Regular reports track the goal we agree, such as enquiries, messages or website visits, with notes on what to test next.

FAQs

Meta Ads questions

Who sets the ad budget?

You do. We structure campaigns around it and review spend with you.

Can you make the ad creative?

We write creative briefs and coordinate assets. Design or video production is a separate scope unless agreed.

Will my ads be approved?

Meta reviews every ad against its policies, so approval cannot be guaranteed. We plan ads to follow the published rules.
