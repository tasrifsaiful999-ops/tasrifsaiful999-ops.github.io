TikTok Account Management

We plan and operate your TikTok presence: video concepts, posting schedule, profile optimisation and performance review. Filming is done by you or a producer you appoint.

Enquire about TikTok Management →WhatsApp

Who it is for

Brands that want an active TikTok channel without building the routine from scratch.

Problems it addresses

  • No idea which formats suit the brand
  • Inconsistent posting
  • Profiles missing a clear bio and link

Scope

What you get

  1. Channel audit and profile setup
  2. Video concept lists and short scripts
  3. Posting schedule and publishing of supplied videos
  4. Caption, sound and hashtag suggestions
  5. Performance review of posted videos

Workflow

How it works with you

You stay involved at each stage and approve the work before it goes live.

  1. Understand

    We learn about your business, audience and goals first.

  2. Plan

    We agree priorities, scope and a calendar with you.

  3. Create & Launch

    We produce the agreed work and publish it with your approval.

  4. Review & Improve

    We report what happened and use it to improve the next cycle.

What we need from you

  • Account access
  • Video files or a filming plan
  • Brand and product guidelines

Reporting and review

A monthly review covers views, watch behaviour and engagement, with concepts for the following month.
