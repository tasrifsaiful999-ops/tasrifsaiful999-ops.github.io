YouTube Channel Management

We organise the channel side of YouTube: channel setup, video titles and descriptions, thumbnail briefs, publishing and analytics review. Video production and editing are outside the default scope.

Enquire about YouTube Management →WhatsApp

Who it is for

Businesses and educators who publish, or plan to publish, video and want a steady channel.

Problems it addresses

  • Videos published without search-friendly titles and descriptions
  • Channel pages that do not explain what viewers will get
  • No routine for reviewing analytics

Scope

What you get

  1. Channel setup and branding checklist
  2. Title, description and tag work for each upload
  3. Thumbnail and playlist briefs
  4. Upload scheduling
  5. Analytics review and content recommendations

Workflow

How it works with you

You stay involved at each stage and approve the work before it goes live.

  1. Understand

    We learn about your business, audience and goals first.

  2. Plan

    We agree priorities, scope and a calendar with you.

  3. Create & Launch

    We produce the agreed work and publish it with your approval.

  4. Review & Improve

    We report what happened and use it to improve the next cycle.

What we need from you

  • Channel access
  • Finished videos
  • Topics or a content plan

Reporting and review

A monthly analytics review covers views, watch time and audience sources, with suggestions for upcoming videos.
