Social Media Content Planning

How to plan consistent, useful content without running out of ideas.

By Saiful Islam Mollah, Digital Marketing Specialist · October 12, 2026

Why plan content

Planning keeps your posts consistent, on-brand and connected to business goals instead of being last-minute.

Choose content pillars

Pick three to five themes you will return to, such as product showcases, behind the scenes, education, customer questions and offers. Example: a fashion brand might rotate styling tips, new arrivals and how-to-order guides.

Build a simple calendar

Decide how often you can post consistently, then assign a pillar and format to each slot. A realistic schedule you keep beats an ambitious one you abandon.

Write captions that help

Lead with the point, keep the language natural to your audience and end with one clear next step.

Review and adjust

Each month, note which posts earned saves, comments or enquiries, and plan more of what works.

Checklist

  • Choose 3–5 content pillars
  • Set a realistic posting frequency
  • Draft one month of topics
  • Write captions with one call to action
  • Review performance monthly
